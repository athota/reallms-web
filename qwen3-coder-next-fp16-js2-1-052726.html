About REALLMS

Research and Academic LLM Services (REALLMS) is Indiana University's comprehensive large language model (LLM) platform, providing powerful AI capabilities to support research, teaching, and learning across all IU campuses.

Located in IU's secure on-premises data centers, REALLMS offers a robust, scalable infrastructure that enables IU researchers and academic personnel to leverage state-of-the-art AI technologies without the concerns of cloud computing or the costs of maintaining private AI systems.

Whether you're conducting text analysis, data processing, language translation, or other AI-powered research, REALLMS provides the computational power and academic support you need to succeed.

  • Powered by cutting-edge large language models
  • Hosted on IU's high-performance on-premises infrastructure
  • Available at no cost to all IU faculty, staff, and students
  • Designed specifically for academic and research applications
  • Committed to data security and privacy standards
